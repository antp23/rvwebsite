About the Park

Welcome to your peaceful desert retreat in historic Chloride, Arizona. Our quiet, long-term friendly RV park is nestled in a charming small town with a rich mining history, offering the perfect escape from the hustle and bustle.

Ideal for snowbirds, retirees, and off-grid travelers seeking tranquility, stunning mountain views, and authentic Arizona living. Experience the beauty of the desert while enjoying modern conveniences and a welcoming community.
