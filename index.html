Welcome to Chloride Western RV Park

Family-owned and under new ownership, Chloride Western RV Park is a peaceful place to rest, relax, and enjoy the Arizona desert. We offer a quiet, friendly environment where travelers are treated like neighbors.

Whether you're staying overnight, for a week, or for the season, our focus is simple: clean facilities, honest rates, and a good night's rest. We live nearby and are always happy to help make your stay comfortable.
